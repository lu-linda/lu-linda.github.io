I am a third-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at Cornell University. I am very lucky to be advised by [Karthik Sridharan](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~sridharan/). My research interests are in theoretical machine learning, particularly in the design of trustworthy learning algorithms with theoretical guarantees. My research was supported by a 2025 [Bowers-CIS Linkedin Fellowship](https://cis.cornell.edu/eight-scholars-awarded-cornell-bowers-cis-linkedin-grants). Recently, I have been working on new approaches to privacy and machine unlearning.

Before joining Cornell, I received a B.S. in Computer Science and a B.S. in Economics from the University of Pennsylvania. I was a member of the [Jerome Fisher Management and Technology Program](https://fisher.wharton.upenn.edu/).
